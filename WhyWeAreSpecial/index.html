😓 The Reality

Why Most Restaurant AI Projects Fail

You already have the data. Why is building an AI layer on top of it so hard?

⏰

Months of Backend Dev

Building custom integrations, RAG pipelines, and new UIs takes months. By the time it launches, your menus or workflows have changed.

👥

Forcing Humans to Chat

Traditional chatbots force managers to type everything. Restaurant workflows are complex and require quick selections, not typing.

🔧

LLM Hallucinations

Letting an LLM generate prices or order statuses causes disasters. You need a single source of truth for business data.

🔄

30-Second Timeouts

If an inventory update or report generation takes 2 minutes, standard chatbots just fail. You need true async support.

✨ The AI Layer

This Is How AI Agents Should Work

You keep your backend. We give you the intelligent orchestration layer.

API-First AI Agents

Connect your existing CRMs. The AI agent maps intents to your backend workflows, handling auth and data transformation.

Auto-UI Generation

Don't force users to chat. The AI generates custom UX screens (forms, tables, buttons) directly from your API responses.

Async & Human-in-the-Loop

Pause workflows for manual approvals. Handle long-running background tasks with async webhooks and update the UI instantly.

Zero Data Storage
