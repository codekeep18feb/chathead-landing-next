The AI Runtime for Restaurant Tech

Built for Restaurant CRMs, franchise platforms, and POS systems with heavy backend APIs. Add a deterministic AI layer without rewriting your backend — or your business.

Use Cases

Restaurant CRMs

Plug deterministic AI agents into Petpooja, Posist, Rista and similar platforms. Turn existing POS, inventory, and payment APIs into intelligent workflows with auto-generated UI — without rewriting your backend.

Franchise Management

Multi-tenant AI agents across hundreds of locations. Granular RBAC, per-store async workflows, and audit trails — all scoped through the opt-in attachment model so no store ever crosses another store's boundary.

POS & Order Workflows

Deterministic order status, refunds, and inventory updates. Each workflow chains multiple POS endpoints with fallback branches — so a 409 from inventory never blocks a refund approval.

Menu & Item Rules

Per-item product flags (ignore-discount, order-type, day-of-week, ignore-tax) are enforced by the runtime before any AI action. A water bottle flagged ignore-discount is skipped when the agent applies a discount — every time, with no hallucination.

Staff & Shift Management

Managers swap shifts and update rosters via natural language. The AI generates a form with available staff, then triggers your existing HR API. Human-in-the-loop pauses handle conflict approvals.

Inventory & Supply Chain

Store staff update stock via chat or forms. Long-running supplier syncs run through async webhooks — no 30-second timeouts, no silent failures, no dropped inventory events.

Refunds & Payment Approvals

AI detects refund intent, extracts the amount, and pauses the workflow for a Manager approval before hitting the payment gateway. Every step is audit-logged and role-gated.

Reservations & Bookings

Multi-step API chaining checks availability, holds the table, and confirms payment. Async webhooks handle slow POS responses without timing out the user session.

Multi-Brand Operations

Manage multiple restaurant brands from one runtime. Each brand gets its own attachment model, RBAC hierarchy, and white-labeled UI — while sharing a single operations backbone.

Restaurant OS / CRM Platforms

Enterprise Restaurant Chains

Multi-brand, multi-country deployments with dedicated SLAs, BYO storage, and custom data residency. Enterprise Activation includes hands-on API mapping and workflow implementation with your team.

Startup Restaurant SaaS

Ship a real AI agent in days, not months. Start free with a working pilot, upgrade to Runtime when you're rolling out to your first paying restaurants — no sales call, no procurement cycle.

Agencies & Integrators

Build AI layers for multiple restaurant clients from one dashboard. Attachment model lets you define golden workflows once and explicitly share them across client apps — with fail-hard guards on cross-tenant changes.

POS & Vendor Integrators

Connect POS, delivery, and payment vendors into one AI layer. Each vendor integration is a first-class node in your workflows — with deterministic fallback and async support for slow endpoints.

Delivery & Aggregator Platforms

Bring Swiggy, Zomato, and direct-order channels under one AI orchestration layer. Aggregate order status from multiple sources into a single deterministic response.
